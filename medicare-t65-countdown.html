Why The Window Matters

Missing it can cost you later.

Part B

Delaying enrollment without other creditable coverage can mean a permanent monthly premium increase - roughly 10% for each full 12-month period you wait.

Part D

A similar permanent late-enrollment penalty can be added to your monthly premium, based on how long you went without creditable drug coverage.

This is why timing your enrollment matters - even if you're not ready to pick a plan yet.

These are general Medicare rules, not a personalized quote or cost calculation. Exact timing and penalties depend on your circumstances, including whether you have creditable coverage such as an active employer plan.
