Why The Window Matters

Missing it can cost you later.

These are general Medicare rules, not a personalized quote or cost calculation. Exact timing and penalties depend on your individual circumstances, including whether you have creditable coverage such as an active employer plan.

Part B penalty

Medicare Part B may carry a permanent premium penalty of about 10% for each full 12-month period you go without Part B after becoming eligible, unless you qualify for a Special Enrollment Period or have other creditable coverage.

Part D penalty

Part D prescription drug coverage has a similar lifetime penalty. It is generally calculated at roughly 1% of the national base premium for each month you go without creditable drug coverage.
