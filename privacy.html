Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2025

VitaLink (“we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the VitaLink mobile application.

Information We Collect

  • Agent and client onboarding details.

How We Use Information

  • To provide secure storage and access to your data.
  • To enable sharing with authorized agents or providers.
  • To send notifications and service updates.

Data Ownership

You retain full ownership of all data you enter into VitaLink. We do not claim ownership of your personal, medical, or insurance information.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. Information may be shared only:

  • With your explicit consent.
  • As required by law or regulation.
  • With trusted third-party service providers under strict data protection agreements.

Third-Party Services

VitaLink uses third-party services such as cloud hosting, analytics, and push notifications (e.g., Firebase). These providers may process limited data necessary to operate the App.

If you join the VitaLink Reach early access list, we collect the contact and business information you submit, such as your name, work email, phone number, licensed state, approximate client count, and early pricing interest. We use this information to evaluate early access requests, contact you about VitaLink Reach, and manage the release process.

VitaLink Reach may process communication-related information such as client contact details, message templates, message status, consent records, opt-out records, inbound reply flags, and audit logs. Agents are responsible for having permission to contact their clients and for using VitaLink Reach in compliance with applicable texting, email, insurance, and privacy rules.

We may use third-party providers, including email delivery services such as Resend and hosting or database providers such as Netlify and Supabase, to operate the website, early access form, and communication workflows.

Data Security

We use encryption and industry-standard safeguards to protect your data. However, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

HIPAA & Medical Information

VitaLink is a user-controlled data storage and sharing platform and is not a healthcare provider, insurer, or covered entity under HIPAA. You are responsible for how you store and share your information.

Data Retention

We retain your data only while your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

Your Choices

  • You may update or delete your information within the app.
  • You may disable notifications in your device settings.
  • You may request full account deletion by contacting us.

Children’s Privacy

VitaLink is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the App indicates acceptance of updates.
