Frequently Asked Questions

Does it text from MY number?

Yes. Every message sends from your personal phone number. Clients know it's you.

Is it built for TCPA compliance?

Yes. VitaLink Reach is built around text opt-in, STOP handling, consent timestamps, and agent responsibility. Agents are still responsible for using it with appropriate client relationships and permission.

What phones does it work on?

Currently Android only. iOS coming soon.

Do my clients need the app?

No. They receive normal text messages on any phone.

Can I use my own email address?

Yes. Connect via Resend and campaigns send from your own address.

When can I get access?

VitaLink Reach is not publicly open yet. Agents will be invited in phases as Android, iOS, onboarding, and support are ready.

Is this part of VitaLink?

Yes. VitaLink Reach is part of the VitaLink platform family. Use it standalone or with other VitaLink tools for a complete agent platform.

Responsible Use

VitaLink Reach is designed for licensed insurance agents communicating with existing clients, warm referrals, and legitimate business relationships. It is not built for blasting cold lists, purchased lead lists, or people who have not given permission to be contacted.

The platform is designed with consent capture, opt-out handling, STOP processing, send limits, and audit records, but each agent remains responsible for following TCPA, CAN-SPAM, carrier rules, state insurance rules, and any carrier or agency compliance requirements that apply to their business.
