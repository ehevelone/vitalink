A safer pause between scan and open

Three steps. You stay in control.

GuarDog never treats a QR code like an automatic command. It decodes first, checks links away from your phone, and waits for your decision.

01

03

More than website links

Understand the code before it acts.

A QR code can do far more than open a webpage. GuarDog identifies common formats and describes the intended action in ordinary language.

URL

Website links

Shows the real destination, redirects, reputation result, and detected warning signs.

Wi-Fi

Network logins

Shows the network name and security type before exposing or copying a password.

SMS

Text messages

Explains who the message targets and previews the prepared message body.

TEL

Phone calls

Displays the number and warns about premium-rate or unusual destinations.

VCF

Contact cards

Lists the contact details and flags hidden links or suspicious impersonation.

TXT

Plain text and more

Displays readable content without assuming it should be launched or trusted.

Built around a simple promise

Your phone should not have to visit a link just to find out what it is.

  • Web links are checked by an isolated service, away from your phone.
  • Your scan history stays on your device unless you clear it or uninstall.
  • Readable links are not placed in long-term analytics logs.
  • Repeat scan results may be cached for up to three hours, then expire automatically.
  • Google Play handles payment details; GuarDog never receives your card number.

An honest security promise

GuarDog checks for known threats and common warning signs. It cannot guarantee that any destination is safe, and a clean result is not permission to ignore your own judgment.

That is why the final choice always belongs to you.
