Frequently Asked Questions

What is health information management?

Health information management is the process of organizing important health details so they are easier to access, update, and share when needed.

How does VitaLink manage health information?

VitaLink gives users one secure place to organize medications, doctors, insurance information, emergency details, and medical history.

Why use one secure place for health information?

Keeping information in one secure place can reduce paperwork, speed up access, and make communication easier during appointments or emergencies.
