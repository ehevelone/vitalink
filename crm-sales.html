Frequently asked questions.

Do my clients need VitaLink?

No. VitaLink CRM works standalone as a complete client management tool. The VitaLink app connection is a powerful bonus when clients use it.

Can I import my existing clients?

Yes. Import from CSV in seconds. Export anytime too — your data is always yours.

How is this different from MAX?

MAX is your upline's platform. VitaLink CRM is yours — independent, portable, and connected to the VitaLink client app ecosystem.

Does it work with VitaLink Reach?

Yes. VitaLink CRM and VitaLink Reach share the same client data. Notes and tasks created in Reach sync to the CRM automatically. One ecosystem, one login.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

Export your complete client list anytime via CSV. Your data is always accessible and portable.

Yes — 14 days full access. No credit card required.
