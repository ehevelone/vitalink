What is VitaLink?

VitaLink is a Personal Health Organization Platform and Health & Insurance ID app available at myvitalink.app.

VitaLink is a secure platform designed to organize important health and insurance information in one place.

Most people keep medications, doctors, insurance cards, emergency contacts, and medical details scattered across papers, wallets, text messages, portals, and multiple apps. VitaLink brings that information together so it is easier to find, update, and share when it matters.

Medications

Keep medication names, doses, frequency, and pharmacy details organized.

Doctors

Store provider names, specialties, phone numbers, and important care contacts.

Insurance

Track insurance cards, policy details, and important coverage information.

Emergency Profile

Prepare critical information for situations where communication may not be possible.

One Secure Place

VitaLink centralizes health and insurance details into one secure location so users, caregivers, and emergency contacts can find the information they need faster.

It is built for everyday organization and for urgent moments when seconds matter.

Prepared. Protected. Connected.

VitaLink helps make personal health information easier to manage before an appointment, during a coverage review, or in an emergency.
