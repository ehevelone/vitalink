What is VitaLink?

VitaLink is a client-controlled health and insurance information platform built for individuals, families, caregivers, and insurance professionals, including Medicare agents. It replaces scattered paperwork and repeated data entry with one place clients maintain themselves, and can choose to share with their agent.

VitaLink is a Personal Health Organization Platform and Health & Insurance ID app available at myvitalink.app.

VitaLink is a secure platform designed to organize important health and insurance information in one place.

For Medicare agents, health insurance agents, and other insurance professionals, VitaLink helps clients keep the information needed for annual reviews easier to update and share when authorized.

Most people keep medications, doctors, insurance cards, emergency contacts, and medical details scattered across papers, wallets, text messages, portals, and multiple apps. VitaLink brings that information together so it is easier to find, update, and share when it matters.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is VitaLink?

VitaLink is a secure Personal Health Organization Platform that helps organize important health and insurance information in one place.

What information can VitaLink store?

VitaLink can store medications, doctors, insurance cards, policy details, emergency contacts, medical history, and emergency profile information.

Who is VitaLink for?

VitaLink is for people who want their health and insurance information easier to organize, access, and share when needed.

Is VitaLink built for Medicare insurance agents?

VitaLink works for any health insurance agent, and is especially useful for Medicare agents managing annual reviews and Annual Enrollment Period outreach. Clients maintain their own medication, doctor, and insurance information and choose when to share updates with their agent.

What type of insurance agent will VitaLink be useful for?

VitaLink is especially valuable for agents whose clients' health information is part of the relationship - including Medicare agents (Medicare Advantage and Medicare Supplement), health insurance agents, final expense and life insurance agents, long-term care insurance agents, and financial advisors managing client health and insurance details. VitaLink isn't limited to health information, though - clients can store any important card or document, including driver's licenses and other identification, so property and casualty agents and other lines can also put it to use. Any agent who benefits from clients keeping accurate, current information handy can use VitaLink alongside their existing CRM.
