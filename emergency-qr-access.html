Emergency QR Access

VitaLink is a Personal Health Organization Platform available at myvitalink.app.

Emergencies can happen when someone is unconscious, unable to speak, confused, disoriented, or involved in an accident. VitaLink helps users prepare a secure emergency profile before that moment arrives.

Create an emergency profile.

Users complete important health and contact information inside VitaLink.

Generate a unique QR.

The user receives a QR that connects to their emergency profile.

Emergency personnel scan the QR.

A secure webpage displays critical information that can help in urgent situations.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does VitaLink Emergency QR Access work?

A user creates an emergency profile, receives a unique QR, and that QR can open a secure emergency information page when scanned.

What can emergency responders see?

The emergency profile can include allergies, medications, medical conditions, implanted devices, blood type, and emergency contacts.

When is emergency QR access useful?

It can help when someone is unconscious, confused, disoriented, injured, or unable to communicate important health information.
