Office-managed access

How RSM/Office-Managed Access Works

When your office covers VitaLink access for your agents, your organization's RSM controls that access - not the individual agent alone.

If an agent's employment or contract ends, an RSM can deactivate that agent's login immediately from the RSM dashboard. Client records tied to that agent's account remain intact; they are not deleted or altered when access is deactivated.

Current functionality

What RSMs Can Do Today

This reflects current VitaLink functionality, not future roadmap copy.

  • View a roster of all agents under their organization
  • Deactivate an agent's login access without deleting the account
  • Reactivate a previously deactivated agent
  • Export an individual agent's client data
  • Choose office-paid billing or agent-paid billing
  • Enroll new agents through an invite link, QR code, or generated unlock code
  • Request client reassignment as a manual support process when needed
Important boundaries

What This Does Not Currently Do

VitaLink does not currently provide full CRM-style browsing of an individual agent's client list from the RSM dashboard. RSM access is roster, billing, activation, and export focused.

Client reassignment is not currently a self-service dashboard action. If a deactivated agent's clients need to be reassigned to another agent, that is handled as a manual request.

The RSM dashboard deactivates and reactivates access. It does not delete agent accounts.
