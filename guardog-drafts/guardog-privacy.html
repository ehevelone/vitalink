This Privacy Policy explains what the VitaLink GuarDog app ("GuarDog", "we", "us") collects, how we use it, and your choices. It is part of the VitaLink family of services operated by [LEGAL ENTITY NAME].

What we collect

  • Your phone number. You sign in with your mobile number, verified by a text‑message code, using Google Firebase Authentication. Your phone number is sent to and stored by Google to verify you and to help prevent spam and abuse. Standard text‑message (SMS) rates from your carrier may apply. We use your number as your account identity and to grant access, including free access or Family‑plan access someone gives you.
  • Camera (to read QR codes). GuarDog uses your camera only to recognize QR codes. Camera frames are processed on your device to read the code; they are not uploaded to us or saved.
  • Links and codes you scan. When you scan a web link, we send it to our checking service to analyze it. Our error logs do not keep the link, and our service statistics store only one‑way hashes of an account identifier and the link, together with the result (for example, "caution"), its reputation status, and the redirect count — never the readable link or your phone number. To speed up repeat scans, the checking service temporarily caches the scan result — which includes the link's destination and any redirect addresses — for up to three (3) hours, after which it is deleted automatically. We do not build a lasting history of your scans on our servers.
  • Your scan history. The list of your past scans is stored only on your phone, not on our servers. You can clear it at any time in the app.
  • Subscription information. If you subscribe, we receive and store a Google Play purchase token (encrypted) and your subscription status, so we can confirm your access. Payment card details are handled by Google Play, not by us.
  • Family‑plan and owner‑granted phone numbers. If you buy a Family plan or are given free access, the phone numbers added are stored so we can grant those accounts access.
  • Device‑integrity signal. We use Firebase App Check to confirm requests come from the genuine app, which helps block abuse.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not read your contacts, photos, location, or messages.
  • We do not open scanned links on your phone — the checking service opens them in isolation so your device doesn't have to.

Services we share data with

To run GuarDog, limited data is processed by:

  • Google Firebase — phone sign‑in, app‑integrity checks, and storing your subscription/access records.
  • Cloudflare — runs the isolated link‑checking sandbox and our backend.
  • Google Web Risk — to check a link's reputation, the link's final destination is sent to Google's threat‑reputation service.
  • Google Play Billing — processes subscription payments and tells us your subscription status.

These providers handle data under their own terms. We share only what is needed for the feature involved.

How long we keep it

We keep your account identity and subscription records while your account exists. Temporarily cached scan results expire automatically within three (3) hours. On‑device scan history stays until you clear it or uninstall the app.

Pseudonymous service statistics. We may retain cryptographic hashes of an account identifier and scanned URL, together with the verdict, reputation status, and redirect count. These records do not contain your readable phone number or URL, but may allow repeated activity involving the same account or URL to be recognized. We keep them to monitor and improve the service.

Deleting your account and data

In the app, open Account and choose Delete GuarDog account. This removes your sign‑in account, your subscription and access records, your trial record, any free‑access grant tied to your number, and your membership in any family plan (and, for a plan you bought, the members you added). Temporarily cached scan results expire on their own within three (3) hours. Pseudonymous service statistics, which cannot be tied back to you, may remain.

If you can't open the app, email myvitalink@outlook.com with the subject "GuarDog account deletion request" and include the phone number you used to sign in. We will delete your account and its associated data and confirm by reply. Instructions are also posted at our account‑deletion page.

Deleting your GuarDog account does not cancel a Google Play subscription — cancel that separately in Google Play. To clear your on‑device scan history, use the clear option in the app or uninstall it.

Children

GuarDog is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above.
