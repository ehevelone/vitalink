1. What GuarDog does

GuarDog helps you preview what is behind a QR code or link before you open it. When you scan a web link, GuarDog opens it in an isolated, separate service ("the sandbox") — not on your phone — follows where it leads, checks it against common warning signs and a reputation service, and shows you a result: a green, yellow, or red indication with the reasons. For contact cards, Wi‑Fi codes, phone numbers, texts, emails, and map locations, GuarDog shows you the details first and never acts on them without your tap.
