With VitaLink

  5. Quotes
Integration opportunity

Client-authorized information should not have to be typed twice.

The opportunity is simple: current information moves from the client to the agent with permission, then becomes available to the existing workflow instead of being rebuilt by hand.

VitaLink is not trying to replace quoting platforms, enrollment systems, carrier workflows, or distribution tools. It helps the client and agent arrive with better information.
